# Every hole in a floor has a cause (roadmap 265)

**The ask.** The walker, 2026-10-19, looking at a level: "why are there holes in the floor?" -- the drop routes Deli
Counter cuts square through a slab. Offered a choice, they chose to keep the routes and give each a reason to exist
("go with 1, give them a reason to exist"), then said what the reason is for: "if we have holes it should be clear to
the player that they are there, but also some reason other than a perfectly cut square (what is diegetic but a signal
to the human playing the game)".

**What shipped.** Zoo 1.114.0 (`patches/patch_zoo_hole_causes.py`) and Deli Counter 0.236.0
(`patches/patch_dc_hole_causes.py`). A drop's CAUSE is decided in Deli Counter (`hole_causes.py`): the link's own
`cause`; `stair` when the hole opens into a stair's well; `hatch` for a hatch link; else a row of `RULES` by the rooms
above and below -- the factory's calls under the walker's direction, meant to be changed. What stands round it is
Zoo's: a torn `hole_edge` round the cut and a `hole_spoil` where the drop lands for `cut` and `broken`, the crew's
`hole_kit` beside a `cut`, and `roof_hatch`'s `floor` form, with the `hatch` interactive, for a `hatch`. The form
names the cause, the floor's construction (`_slab` under a concrete floor: bars, not joists) and the ceiling below
(`_plaster`: plaster and lath, not tiles). Nothing new collides but the kit and the hatch: the slab's cut stays the
collider and the drop's route.

  over its cashier cage, the precincts' armory over the garage, the deli preset's drop to the stairwell -- clear of

The drop's edge has room: the nearest built wall face to a hole's edge is 0.35 m above (the walk-ups' bedroom) and
0.225 m below (the delis' kitchen wall, where 0.235.0 slid the hole), so the edge's 0.30 m band reaches at most 0.075 m
into a wall's 0.30 m and never out of its far face.

## Found on the way, kept

- **The four stair-well hatches.** The first build stood a floor hatch round deli_a01-a03's and rowhouse_raid's
  hatch links, and `circulation.check_shell` read each frame 0.03 m inside the stair's column (`circ_ab_*`). The
  links are cut inside the stair's own well -- there is no floor there to frame. `hole_causes.stair_share` now gives
  such a hole the cause `stair` and nothing stands round it; one that only partly reaches into a well (rowhouse_raid,
  70 %) is named by L28. On the twelve specs built with 0.235.0 and with 0.236.0, no new conflict in any
  (`circ_ab_0235_vs_0236.out.txt`); foundry_heist_vertical's 2 and warehouse_a02's 1 are 0.235.0's.
- **A slot Zoo cannot build is a box.** `kit._species_fit` builds a slot outside its species' sizes as the plain box at
  the slot's size, which over a hole would cover it. Deli Counter mirrors the four genomes' sizes (`GENOME_DIMS`) and
  leaves such a slot out; nothing in the library comes near (the widest hole 5 x 3 m against the edge's 6.8 m).
- **Two float tie-breaks.** All four sides of a centred hole were 1.8 m from the room's centre and float noise
  (-3.9 + 0.7 against -6.1 - 0.7) chose the kit's side; a spoil cut back to 2 cm off a piece and re-tested at 2 cm
  met it again by 4e-16 and was dropped. Distances are now compared to the millimetre and the cut-back is tested at
  1 cm. The same rounding moved the six delis' floor-hatch lid from south to north: their hatch is centred between two
  walls, so float noise had chosen and now the side's name does.
- **Zoo's spoil, three defects** (Zoo's CHANGELOG, "Found before it shipped"): a bar "bent" by moving the far end of
  an eight-vertex box was set askew past its plan's width, onto a fragment; pieces placed row by row left a small slot
  without the ceiling's pieces -- the walk-ups' 1.7 x 1.83 m spoil had no ceiling tile in three variants of four; and
  the floor's finish rode only the sawn panels, so a `broken` floor's boards came down bare whatever the stock.
- **This census's own error.** The per-form census first set the stock on the edge as well; the edge takes none, Zoo
  keeps a slot's dressing all or nothing, and it built the plain edge -- read off the stems before any number was
  used (`patches/zoo_hole_causes/census_forms.py` now sets it on the spoil alone).

## The first cold run: the greybox over the hole (9340)

Cold run 9340 (restaurant_row_001, seed 9003, Deli Counter 0.236.0 and Zoo 1.114.0; `docs/cold_runs/cold_9340/`) was 0
interventions and read 8 new findings, every one `WALKTEST_WALKER_STUCK`: all four walkers on both of deli_a03's
candidates sent to a target inside the deli's drop, stuck in its north-west corner on the slab's cut faces. One
cause: 0.236.0 drew the edge's greybox as one box over the hole, its top 12 mm above the floor, and Lot bakes the walk
test's navmesh from the candidate's greybox lot with visual meshes in it (`geometry_parsed_geometry_type = 2`), so the
bake took the box for floor. `greybox_over_holes.py` measured the class over the library: on 0.236.0 every dressed
hole, 16, had greybox over its whole area, 56.76 m2 in all (a floor hatch's box did the same); Deli Counter 0.236.1
draws a ring of four boxes outside the hole instead, their union still the slot's footprint, and none is covered
(`greybox_over_holes.out.txt`). The roof hatch's box over its roof hole (`roof_hatch.py`, 0.224.0) is the same shape
and was not examined here.

Its frames are the first look at a hole in a level: `docs/cold_runs/cold_9340/holes_9339_vs_9340.jpg`, the same
stations as 9339's in the level's light. The hole reads as broken through, not cut square, from above and from the
kitchen; the kit stands beside it with its light aimed in. Two things seen and left for the walker: the spoil reads
sparse at play distance, and the kit's 0.35 m off the south rim costs two of the hole's declared drop edges.
