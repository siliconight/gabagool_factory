# The responders' cruiser: the walker's comps, and the calls made

The walker, 2026-10-08, answering roadmap 212's ask for a 1990s police
cruiser and an invented department:

> I would think a classic 1990s Crown Victoria

> Delco County Police Dept. as a start?

Six photographs came with it: five exteriors, then one interior. They were
shown in chat and are not stored here. What follows is what was read off
them, format only. No real department's name, seal, number or plate is
reproduced. Roadmap 212 tracks the work.

## THE CALLS

- **The car:** a classic 1990s Ford Crown Victoria police sedan, the long
  three-box full-size.
- **The department:** DELCO COUNTY POLICE DEPT. "Delco" is the county's
  own slang, and no real department carries the name.
- **The livery is not called yet.** Among the five liveried cars, two are
  black with white doors, one is black with a cream roof, and two are white
  with blue stripes. The first frames should show a black-and-white and a
  white-with-blue-stripe version.

## WHAT THE EXTERIORS SHOW

Each line is one photograph, read for format.

- **A state trooper's car, and a retired one beside it.**
  - The trooper's car: a 1998-or-later body, black with a cream roof (the
    doors stay black), the agency's name in gold on the front doors, a
    round seal on the rear door, a low silver bar of lamps on the roof, and
    a heavy black push bar across the nose.
  - The retired car: all black, steel wheels with small centre caps, an
    A-pillar spotlight on the driver's side, and the lamps and markings
    stripped.
- **A city car on a brick rowhome street:** the nearest to Delco.
  - The car: a 1998-or-later body, all white, one broad blue stripe along
    each side at the belt line, the city's name in script inside it, and a
    unit number on the front fender.
  - The roof: a red-and-blue bar.
  - The street: wires overhead and a corner store.
- **A 1992-97 body, as a model and as the real car:** the rounder,
  smooth-nosed first generation.
  - The paint: black with white doors.
  - The doors: a gold seal and a motto in two lines.
  - The rear: the city's name on the rear fender, and a red-and-blue bar on
    the roof.
- **A 2000s car on a beach:**
  - The paint: black with white doors and roof, and "POLICE" in large
    letters across both doors.
  - The numbers: an emergency number on the rear fender, and a unit number
    on the tail.
  - The roof: a low modern bar.
  - The wheels: black steel with small chrome caps.
- **A 1998-or-later car abroad:** all white, blue stripes on the hood and
  sides, "POLICE" on the hood and doors, and a tall round-ended
  red-and-blue bar on the roof.

## WHAT THE INTERIOR SHOWS

A 1990s police-package interior seen through the driver's door:
- **Seats:** two front buckets in grey-blue cloth, with a fold-down
  armrest.
- **The dash:** grey, with a column shifter, a small plain gauge cluster,
  and square vents.
- **Between the seats:** a black equipment console carrying a radio head,
  with a coiled microphone cord hanging from it.
- **The floor and sill:** dark blue carpet, and a ribbed sill plate in the
  door opening.

## WHAT EVERY COMP AGREES ON

These are the parts that make the car read as a police Crown Victoria
whatever the livery:
- **The body:** a long, low, wide three-box sedan with a long hood and
  trunk, four doors, and a near-vertical C-pillar with a small quarter
  window.
- **The roof bar:** a lamp bar across the roof over the B-pillars, red on
  one side and blue on the other.
- **Push bar and spotlight:** a push bar on the nose, on most of them, and
  an A-pillar spotlight on the driver's side.
- **The wheels:** black steel with small centre caps, on most.
- **The markings:** the department's name on the doors or the fenders, a
  seal on the doors, and a unit number.
