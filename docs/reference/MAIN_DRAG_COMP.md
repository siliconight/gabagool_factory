# Main drag comp: Industrial Highway through Tinicum (the walker, 2026-10-21)

**What the walker sent.** A street map of PA-291, the Industrial Highway, through Essington and Tinicum by the
airport, with I-95's interchange in its north-west corner: "save this for later...do a remix of this street, main
drag, to have a cheesteak shop, bank, coffeshop, wawa ,ect ect for a level comp". The picture is not stored -- it is
a map service's rendering, and comps here are written up, not copied (`PHILLY_STREET_COMPS.md`'s rule). This is what
it shows, read off it, and what a level made from it would need.

## The street as it is

- **The main drag** is a four-lane arterial running west-south-west to east-north-east, with a service road along its
  south side where the businesses front onto it.
- **A freight line runs right behind the drag's south side**, spur tracks fanning out to the east; an old road peels
  off south-east across the tracks.
- **Side streets cross the drag every block** (four avenues in the view) into a tight grid of small residential
  blocks to the north and to the south.
- **On the drag's south side**: a dive club (the walker's club comp -- vibe only, never its name, see
  [the club note in the walker's memory]), a restaurant, and a Wawa-style store on a corner.
- **North of the drag**: a ballfield with a park, a towing yard to the west, airport parking lots to the east, a hot
  dog stand and a pizzeria up the side streets.
- **East and south-east**: a stone yard, trucking and warehousing -- an industrial edge.
- **North-west**: the expressway cloverleaf and a creek, which close the view.

rail line behind as a hard edge and the expressway as the backdrop. Along the drag, every business under an invented
Delco name, PG-13 and crass, never a real one (`fake-delco-brands`): the Wawa is **FLAPPAHS**, the store and gas
forecourt the walker already settled.

## What would make it

   shop and a coffee shop as Deli Counter families, their working fixtures as Zoo species (flat-top and steam table,

3. **The street**: a four-lane arterial with a service road on one side, and the rail line as an edge.
4. **A cold run of the remix**, walked by the walker.
