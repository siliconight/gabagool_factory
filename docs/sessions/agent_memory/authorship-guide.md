The walker handed over a 1,100-line art-direction guide on 2026-10-02 ("adding this if it helps us"). Verbatim copy: `docs/reference/HUMAN_AUTHORSHIP_GUIDE.md`; my reading against the factory: `docs/AUTHORSHIP_GUIDE_APPLIED.md`.

Its north star: "it looks like humans placed these things and made this stuff with passion and care" -- not AAA realism. Core rules: specificity beats detail; every wear mark, bevel and unusual detail needs a cause; quiet space is composition; text is correct or a clean shape; every prominent object answers "why is this here?"; the player camera is the judge; compare against a golden set.

**How to apply:** it constrains [[realism-replaces-retro-look]] -- do not apply grain/edge-darkening/chamfers as a uniform recipe; write a short brief per prop (material, who touches it, what it is for) and let shading follow it. It is the first written definition of "good" for roadmap item 18; candidate instruments listed in the applied doc (text height in camera, duplicate silhouettes per view, density per zone, the section-15 rubric).
