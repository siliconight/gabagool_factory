What it says that matters here: a typeface is assigned to an OWNER (the bank network, the cigarette maker, the state's ticket printer, the shop), few families with real functions, hierarchy by size/weight before another font, respect the physical production method, no novelty faces as body copy, keep a font register (source URL, date, licence file, roles). CC0 must be confirmed on the exact download; OFL/"free" are not CC0.

Candidates it names: Aileron, Vegur, Marius1 (UI/institutional sans); MFB Oldstyle, Seshat, Tenderness (print/documents); Penna (storefront/short labels); Medio, Ferrum (titles only); Pixel Operator (already vendored in Pixelcoat), Public Pixel, C-64, Kenney, SG, Not Jam packs (pixel/terminal).
