The walker gave five grocery-interior comps on 2026-10-26: "shiny floor, bright lights, isles, glowing freezer section, stacked high". The reading and the gap table are filed in docs/reference/GROCERY_STORE_COMPS.md.

**Why:** they read seed 9205's supermarket as "far too dark" and unfinished. The 1990s look is flat, even and bright fluorescent light. The stockroom should be dimmer than the sales floor (about 25 against 100 fc).

**How to apply:** a supermarket's sales floor wants continuous strip runs about 2–3 m apart (the 12-lamp cap binds), a polished pale tile that reflects them, a racetrack stripe, an aisle sign over each aisle, a cooler_run freezer aisle and full shelves.
