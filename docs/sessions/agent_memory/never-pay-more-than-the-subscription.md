The walker (2026-10-15): "I do not want to ever pay more than the fixed monthly cost." They are on a Claude
Max subscription.

**Why:** A Max plan is a fixed fee with usage limits that pause rather than charge, BUT pay-as-you-go "extra
usage" can be switched on in claude.ai Settings -> Billing, and a few features are billed separately (the
session guidance names `/code-review ultra`, the multi-agent cloud review, as user-triggered and billed).

**How to apply:** never trigger anything described as billed, metered, or pay-as-you-go (ultrareview, paid
cloud workflows, extra usage) and never suggest enabling extra usage; if a step would cost money beyond the
plan, say so and stop instead. Long autonomous work only spends the plan's allowance, which pauses at the
limit; that is acceptable. Account and billing settings are the walker's to change, never mine. See also
[[keep-it-rolling]].
