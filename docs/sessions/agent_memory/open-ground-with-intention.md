The walker, 2026-10-24, on the walk of 9386's finding 7 (wide empty ground): "my caution is around its ok for levels to have open areas but it should be with intention and to support gameplay not just because we forgot to put something there". Earlier the same finding: "careful how we solve this one".

**Why:** the land-use guide says urban land is used ([[land-use-guide-informs-lot]]); the game is fights first ([[game-is-a-series-of-shooting-galleries]]). Both hold: open ground that serves a fight is design, open ground nobody chose reads as generated.

**How to apply:**
- Every open area the generator leaves is a NAMED use with a recorded reason in the site (a lot kept open for a sightline, a yard as an approach, a plaza between spawn and objective) and is DRAWN as that use (fence, lot paint, rubble, grass) -- never bare remainder.
- Ground with no gameplay reason gets filled: facing rows of Empties, back yards and alleys, verges.
- Measure both: the remainder (with Empties counted as built land -- the census missed them until 2026-10-24) and the fight space (sightlines, cover) on the same seed before and after.
- Findings and options: docs/findings/walk_2026-10-23_9386/README.md, section 7, and landuse/.

**The tiebreaker, the walker, the same day:** "in doubt, we lean on our documentation of making worlds feel lived in a real, so we connect with the experience as the player". When a call is unclear, decide it by the lived-in/real documents -- `docs/LEVEL_STANDARD.md` ([[level-excellence-standard-doc]]), the authorship guide ([[authorship-guide]]: cause and specificity), the land-use guide `docs/reference/LAND_PRESSURE_AND_SPATIAL_LOGIC.md`, [[lived-in-touches]] -- and say which passage decided it.
