The walker supplied the placement rule for stop signs (2026-09-13), citing
MUTCD-style guidance:

- One sign per approach, on the RIGHT side of the roadway for the approaching
  driver.
- At least 6 ft (1.83 m) from the pavement edge laterally.
- No more than 50 ft (15.2 m) from the intersection.
- About 4 ft (1.2 m) in advance of a marked crosswalk line.
- A second sign on the LEFT only for wide intersections or multi-lane approaches.
- The plate faces the approaching driver with the pole BEHIND the plate, and the
  face carries the white STOP legend.
