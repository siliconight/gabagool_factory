`docs/STREET_RULES.md` in the factory root: the stop sign placement rules
(MUTCD summary) and crosswalk rules (NACTO Urban Street Design Guide) the walker
supplied on 2026-09-13. Key points: every leg of a signalised intersection gets a
marked crosswalk; busier streets (>3,000 ADT, >20 mph, 2+ lanes) mark every
intersection; mid-block signalised crossings at >=200 ft spacing; at-grade,
single-cycle crossings; no porkchop islands; a door footpath is not an approach
and gets no stop sign. Owners: Lot (`site_streets`, `site_furniture`), Level
Factory (spur paths). Related: [[stop-sign-placement-follows-mutcd]].
