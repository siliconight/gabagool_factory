They are two stores and the store should be both: spines-out dense shelves on PAINTED units (purple/green/blue/black), tall double-sided aisles, hand-cut section words on top ("WAR", "CHOP!"), yellow aisle-end signs and shelf tags, teal walls, orange armchairs, a movie one-sheet on a door; plus a chain-style NEW RELEASES wall of faced-out boxes under cut-out letters and a counter in the store's colours with stacks of tapes.

**Why:** these are direction for a store the walker has not yet said they like, so they do revise it (unlike [[references-fill-blanks-not-override]] cases).
